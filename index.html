Marker Workspace

A private tool for planning garment cutting markers and estimating fabric consumption.

Open Marker Workspace

What it does

  • Imports garment pattern pieces from DXF/RUL files, and checks their outlines, grain lines and sizes.
  • Lays the pieces out on a fabric width to make a cutting marker, and independently checks every layout.
  • Estimates fabric length and consumption per garment, and compares widths and size ratios.
  • Exports a job package, an SVG report and inspection evidence, as a download or saved to your Google Drive.

Who can use it

Marker Workspace is a private tool, not a public service. Only Google accounts its owner has invited can sign in; anyone else is refused at Google's sign-in.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

You sign in with your Google account. If you choose Save to Google Drive, the app asks for access to files it creates in your Drive only (Google's drive.file permission). It can't see or change anything else in your Drive. Read the privacy policy for what's stored and where.
